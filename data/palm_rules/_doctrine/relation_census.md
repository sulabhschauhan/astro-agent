# Relation-Machinery Census — All LINE Chapters

**Provenance note (added S97, post-hoc correction):** these census counts were
generated by an AI research fork. The Pattern-B Life-chapter-concentration
claim below, and its correction (9 of the 11 B instances are the Hindu ray-
line / Line of Influence subsystem, already scoped out per
`unauthorable_register.json`'s `line_life` entry and CLAUDE.md's S96 lock),
were independently verified in-chat this session by reading the source
prose directly (fulltext lines 897-921 explicitly name "the Hindus,"
"ray-line(s)," and "the line of influence") and cross-checking
`unauthorable_register.json`. The remaining per-chapter A/C/D counts are
spot-checked only (2 verbatim quotes confirmed against source, the
grouping-summary arithmetic re-added by hand) — **not** exhaustively
re-derived line-by-line. Treat A/C/D totals as **±1** until the chapter
feeding whichever pattern gets built is read in full again.

**Scope:** Cheiro's *Language of the Hand*, all 9 "line" chapters (the complete,
exhaustive set per the book's own line-index at `cheiro_pdf_fulltext.md` lines
789-800): Life, Mars, Head, Heart, Fate, Sun, Health (Hepatica), Intuition (incl.
the Via Lasciva paragraph sharing its chapter), Marriage. Source:
`data/cheiro/cheiro_pdf_fulltext.md`. Chapter XV ("The Girdle of Venus, the Ring
of Saturn, and the Three Bracelets") and Chapter XVII onward (Children/Star/
Cross/Square/etc.) are marks/signs chapters, not line chapters — excluded.

Line ranges read this pass (Fate excluded — see below):
Life 863-929 · Mars 931-943 · Head 945-1033 (incl. Ch. VIII 7-type modifiers and
Ch. IX insanity/murderous-propensities, both still head-line doctrine) · Heart
1035-1073 · Sun 1119-1151 · Health 1153-1176 · Intuition 1178-1182 (incl. Via
Lasciva) · Marriage 1200-1241.

**Method note:** this is a relation-PATTERN TAG SCAN, not a full doctrine
inventory (unlike `doctrine_fate.md`). Only sentences describing one line/
feature's spatial or physical relationship to another named landmark or line
are tagged; non-relational doctrine (hand-type modifiers, marks, soft
qualities, meta/scope prose) is not rowed. Granularity is per relational
clause, matching `doctrine_fate.md`'s per-doctrinal-clause convention.
Page citations use the same pragmatic method as `doctrine_fate.md`: nearest
preceding running-header/footer page marker in the raw OCR text, plumbed
through visible OCR corruption the same way (e.g. `1)6` -> 96) where needed.

**Line of Fate** is fully authored already (`doctrine_fate.md`,
`palm_rules_fate_line_v1.json`) and was NOT re-read this pass. Its
contribution below is the 4 `parked_pending` rows already on record:
- **FT_P04** (p.103, doctrine F017a, "a branch run in and join it from the
  Mount of Luna") — **Pattern A** (incoming branch; direction is Luna ->
  fate line, the reverse of BRANCHES_TO).
- **FT_P03** (p.105, doctrine F030, branch from Luna + branch from Venus
  simultaneously) — **Pattern D** (dual/multi target).
- **FT_P01** (p.104, doctrine F018, "any other mount") and **FT_P02** (p.103,
  doctrine F017b, "on a woman's hand") are **excluded from A-D** — a generic/
  wildcard-target gap and a gender-conditioning gap respectively, neither a
  relation-machinery expressiveness issue.

*Transparency note:* `doctrine_fate.md`'s full 51-row table (not re-derived
here, just cross-referenced) actually contains two further NOT-expressible

## Build-sizing recommendation

**Ranking, corrected for scope: C > A > D > B.** B's raw-11 total is no
longer the tiebreaker it looked like before this correction — 9 of its 11
instances are already-scoped-out doctrine (see above), so it is ranked
LAST by real in-scope demand, not first by raw count.

1. **Pattern C (convergence/joins) — 6 instances, 4 chapters, ALL in-scope.**
   Highest real in-scope yield of the four once B's scoped-out mass is set
   aside. Life, Head, Heart, and Health each carry at least one instance,
   and — notably — 3 of the 6 are independent restatements of the SAME
   "two lines meet, and where they meet matters" doctrine (life/health
   meeting = point of death; head/heart meeting-location = age-of-crime-
   onset). A single symmetric "line A meets line B [at location L]"
   primitive would satisfy this pattern across the widest spread of
   chapters of any of the four, including unlocking Fate's own
   still-parked F025b in the same stroke (not counted in this census's
   totals, but immediately in scope once C exists).
2. **Pattern A (incoming branch) — 4 instances, 3 chapters (Heart, Fate,
   Marriage), ALL in-scope.** The narrowest fix conceptually — it only
   requires flipping BRANCHES_TO's directionality to also accept an
   incoming form (or adding an inverse field) — and it is the pattern
   already flagged as blocking a KNOWN, already-authored-and-parked rule
   (FT_P04). 2 of its 4 instances (Fate + one Marriage restatement) are
   the same doctrine told twice.
3. **Pattern D (dual/multi target) — 3 instances, 3 chapters (Heart, Fate,
   Health), ALL in-scope.** Requires the most invasive schema change (a
   single attribute field accepting 2+ simultaneous targets, echoing the
   already-deferred cheirognomy multi-value work) — the most expensive
   build of the three genuinely in-scope patterns for the smallest
   measured yield.
4. **Pattern B (crossed/stopped-by) — 11 instances raw, but only ~2
   in-scope, 1 chapter (Marriage).** Was ranked #1 by raw count before this
   correction; now ranked LAST. 9 of its 11 instances (82%) are the Hindu
   ray-line/Line of Influence subsystem, already scoped out per
   `unauthorable_register.json`'s `line_life` entry and CLAUDE.md's S96
   lock — this project will not author against that doctrine regardless of
   whether B's machinery gets built. Its remaining real demand (Marriage's
   2 instances) is the smallest genuine in-scope yield of any pattern —
   **mostly scoped-out, likely never built** on current evidence.

**Recommendation: build Pattern C (convergence/joins) first.** C is the
best-distributed in-scope pattern (4 of 9 chapters), its instances are
conceptually a single clean primitive ("line A meets line B, optionally
gated by where"), and it directly unlocks one already-parked,
already-authored Fate row (F025b) the moment it lands — turning a
measurement into an immediate authoring win, not just future capacity. B is
NO LONGER defensible as the #1 pick on raw count once its scope-out is
applied: 9 of its 11 instances sit in a subsection this project has already
ruled out of scope, so building B first would mean investing in machinery
whose largest measured "yield" this project will never actually author
against — worth flagging explicitly so B is not mistaken for "the" #2 build
on the strength of its now-corrected raw-count total.
