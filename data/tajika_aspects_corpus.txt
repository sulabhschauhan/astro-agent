TAJAKA ASPECT YOGAS — INTERPRETIVE CORPUS (Ithasala / Ishrafa, Varshaphal)
==========================================================================

PURPOSE
  Interpretive doctrine the expert interpreter reads to speak to a Tajika
  aspect yoga in the annual (Varshaphal) chart. The ENGINE
  (agent/calculations/annual/tajaka_aspects.py) computes WHICH planet pairs are
  in Ithasala (applying) or Ishrafa (separating), from the deeptamsa orbs and
  the real signed motion; THIS corpus supplies what those yogas MEAN. Pairs
  with the fact-block "tajaka_aspects" entry.

SOURCES
  * B.V. Raman, "Varshaphal — or The Hindu Progressed Horoscope", Chapter VII
    (Yogas, Sahams etc.), §§57–63 — English, verbatim OCR from
    data/pdfs/2015.134416.Varshaphal-Or-The-Hindu-Progressed-Horoscope_djvu.txt
    (djvu line anchors given per segment). The "OCR:" lines quote the source
    as scanned (garbling and all) so every English gloss is checkable against
    it; the "Gloss:" lines are a plain reading of that same text.

STATUS / FIDELITY
  Already-English Raman — NO translation step, so this corpus carries no
  translation risk (same posture as PART 3 of the saham corpus). The only
  liberty taken is repairing obvious OCR garble in the gloss; the OCR line is
  kept verbatim beside it so the repair is auditable. No Sulabh verification
  gate is required for these segments (unlike the Devanagari saham doctrine).

COVERAGE (deliberate, recorded)
  Raman details six yogas (§58–63): Ishkavala, Induvara, Ithasala, Easarapha
  (Ishrafa), Khallasara, Raddha. These cover the engine's PRIMARY predictive
  yogas (Ithasala/Ishrafa) plus the chart-level pair (Ishkavala/Induvara) and
  the two conditions that modify an Ithasala (Khallasara, Raddha). The engine
  ALSO flags Kamboola / Nakta / Yamaya / Manahu, which §57 only NAMES — Raman
  gives no result-doctrine for them, and the Tajika Nilakanthi passage that
  does is Devanagari (translation risk + a verification gate), so their full
  doctrine is a LATER ingest. Until then the fact block labels them
  descriptively and the general Ithasala doctrine below applies to them.

  NOTE (architecture, same as the sahams): Tajika aspects are an ANNUAL

==========================================================================
GENERAL DOCTRINE
==========================================================================

[[seg: taj_gen_framework | domains: * ]]
THE SIXTEEN TAJIKA YOGAS; ITHASALA IS THE KEY. Tajika reads planets by their
DEGREE within a sign, not by whole sign. Of the sixteen special combinations
("Iddasaladi Shodasa Yogas"), Ithasala (applying) and Ishrafa (separating) are
the foundation; most of the others are modifications of them. A matter is read
through the pair of planets that signify it (e.g. the lagna lord and the lord
of the relevant house): whether they are coming together (the matter forms this
year) or drifting apart (it has passed).
  Raman §57 (djvu l.2846–2854), OCR: "THE YOGAS — The Thachakasars gives 16
  yogas or special combinations called Iddasaladi Shodasa Yogas … the most
  important ones … (1) Ishkavala, (2) Induvara, (3) Iddasala [Ithasala],
  (4) Easarapha [Ishrafa], (5) Nakta, … (8) Kamboola, … (11) Raddha, …
  (16) Durupha."

[[seg: taj_gen_deeptamsa | domains: * ]]
THE ORB OF INFLUENCE (DEEPTAMSA). Two planets act on each other only when they
are within each other's "deeptamsa" — a planet-specific orb (Sun 15, Moon 12,
Mars 8, Mercury 7, Jupiter 9, Venus 7, Saturn 9 degrees). The closer the two
planets are in degree, the stronger the effect. Outside the orb, an aspect
between the signs carries no yoga. A strong, dignified, benefic pair gives the
good form of the matter; a weak or malefic pair, the adverse form — the yoga
tells you WHETHER and WHEN, the planets' strength tells you HOW WELL.


==========================================================================
THE APPLYING / SEPARATING PAIR
==========================================================================

[[seg: taj_ithasala | domains: * ]]
ITHASALA — THE APPLYING ASPECT (the matter forms / ripens this year). Formed
when the orbs of a slower and a faster planet overlap and the faster planet is
APPROACHING exactitude. It is the most important and the most AUSPICIOUS of the
Tajika yogas — the promise that the signified matter comes together within the
year. Three kinds: VARTHAMANA (present — the faster planet is behind the slower,
inside the orb), POORNA/SAMA (complete — the two are at the same degree, the
aspect exact), and BHAVISHYA (future — the faster planet is just forward of the
slower, inside the orb, the perfection yet to come).
  Raman §60 (djvu l.2867–2890), OCR: "IDDASALAYOGA — If the Deepthamsas of a
  slow moving planet mix with those of a faster moving planet this yoga is
  caused … Guru's deepthamsas are 9 and Sukra's 7; if Guru and Sukra are
  within 7° from each other this yoga is produced. Three kinds … If a Seegra
  graha (greater velocity) is backwards of a Manda graha (slower velocity)
  within its Deepthamsas [Varthamana]; if a Seegragraha is in the same degree
  as a Mandagraha [Poorna]; and if a Seegragraha is forwards of a Mandagraha
  (within its deepthamsa) [Bhavishya] … Results — based upon the relations
  between the planets causing the yoga and the sign in which it is caused; in
  favourable circumstances good results flow, in unfavourable, evil results."

[[seg: taj_ishrafa | domains: * ]]
ISHRAFA (EASARAPHA) — THE SEPARATING ASPECT (the matter has just passed). When
the faster planet has moved PAST exactitude — just a degree in advance of the
slower — the aspect is separating. It is the inauspicious counterpart of
Ithasala: the opportunity is gone, the effort fails or comes too late.
  Raman §61 (djvu l.2894–2900), OCR: "EASARAPHAYOGA — A Seegragraha, just one
  degree in advance of a Mandagraha, produces this yoga. Results — Failure in
  all business undertakings, unfavourable and producing evil influences at all
  times."
  NOTE on retrograde: direction is judged by REAL motion, so a retrograde
  planet that appears to be applying is in truth separating — an apparent
  Ithasala that is false (the "Khalasara" case). See taj_radda for the related
  spoiling condition.


==========================================================================
THE CHART-LEVEL PAIR
==========================================================================

[[seg: taj_ishkavala | domains: * ]]
ISHKAVALA — a whole-chart yoga of PROSPERITY. Present when the planets occupy
only the kendras (1,4,7,10) and panapharas (2,5,8,11), leaving the apoklimas
(3,6,9,12) empty. It gives wealth, happiness and good fortune for the year.
  Raman §58 (djvu l.2855–2858), OCR: "ISHKAVALAYOGA — Planets in kendras
  (1,4,7,10) and panapharas (2,5,8,11) alone, without any in Apoklimas
  (3,6,9,12), produce this yoga. Results — Accession to wealth, happiness and
  good luck are the results."

[[seg: taj_induvara | domains: * ]]
INDUVARA — the adverse whole-chart yoga. Present when the planets occupy only
the apoklimas (3,6,9,12), the kendras and panapharas empty. It gives
disappointment, mental worry and physical ailments.
  Raman §59 (djvu l.2859–2861), OCR: "INDUVARAYOGA — Planets in Apoklimas,
  without any in the other houses, cause this yoga. Results — Disappointments
  in all undertakings, mental worry and physical ailments."


==========================================================================
CONDITIONS THAT MODIFY AN ITHASALA
==========================================================================

[[seg: taj_khallasara | domains: * ]]
KHALLASARA — a BROKEN connection. When the lagna lord stands between the Moon
and the other planets and there is NO intermingling of their deeptamsas, the
link that an Ithasala would make is absent; the matter is unsupported.
  Raman §62 (djvu l.2901–2905), OCR: "KHALLASARAYOGA — If the lord of Lagna is
  between Chandra and any other planets and if there is no intermingling of the
  Deepthamsas of each with those of the other, then this yoga is produced.
  Results — All unfavourable, disappointments in all undertakings."

[[seg: taj_radda | domains: * ]]
RADDA (RADDHA) — a SPOILED Ithasala. When a planet forming an Ithasala is
RETROGRADE or COMBUST (within the Sun's deeptamsa), it is too weak to deliver:
the Ithasala becomes Raddha instead. The matter is not denied outright, but
comes only after much trouble — final success after trials. (This is the
QUALITY counterpart to the direction rule: retrograde motion reverses
applying/separating, AND a retrograde/combust planet weakens whatever Ithasala
survives.)
  Raman §63 (djvu l.2906–2911), OCR: "RADDHAYOGA — A planet in retrogression or
  combustion and within the fold of Ravi's Deepthamsa, when causing an
  Iddasalayoga, will be utterly powerless to effect the results of such a yoga,
  so that instead of Iddasalayoga Raddhayoga is produced. Results — Final
  success after much trouble and trials in any undertakings."
