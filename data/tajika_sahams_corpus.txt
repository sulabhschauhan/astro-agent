TAJAKA SAHAMS — INTERPRETIVE CORPUS (Varshaphal / annual technique)
====================================================================

PURPOSE
  Interpretive doctrine the expert interpreter reads to speak to a saham's
  placement in the annual (Varshaphal) chart. The ENGINE
  (agent/calculations/annual/sahams.py) computes each saham's longitude/house;
  THIS corpus supplies what that placement MEANS. Pairs with the fact-block
  saham facts.

SOURCES
  * Tajika Nilakanthi (Nilakantha Daivajna), Saham adhyaya —
    data/pdfs/tajika_nilakanthi_hindi_djvu.txt. Line anchors (l.NNNN) are to
    that file. Devanagari key phrases are quoted so each English gloss is
    checkable against the source.
  * B.V. Raman, Varshaphal Ch.VII (§64–76) — English, verbatim OCR, PART 3.

STATUS / FIDELITY
  The English in PART 1 and PART 2 is CLAUDE'S TRANSLATION of OCR'd Devanagari
  (Hindi bhasha-tika). There is no published English edition to grade it
  against, so it is FIDELITY-PENDING until Sulabh (who reads the source)
  verifies it. Spots where the OCR is broken are marked [OCR?]. PART 3 (Raman)
  is already-English and carries no translation risk.
  >>> SULABH VERIFICATION GATE: read each "Nila:" Devanagari line against its
      English gloss before this corpus is trusted in production. <<<

HOW IT IS SELECTED
  Each segment carries a double-bracket seg tag (seg-id plus a domains list),
  of the form shown on every entry below. Ingested into
  chapter_index_bphs.json (one unit "nila_sahams") + domain_tags_bphs.json
  (segment-level), so a marriage question pulls the Vivaha saham + general
  doctrine, a career question pulls Karma/Rajya, etc. "*" = all life-area
  domains (the general doctrine rides along with any saham).
  NOTE (architecture, for Sulabh): sahams are an ANNUAL technique but the
  corpus-selection layer has no natal/annual distinction, so a tag of
  "marriage" will surface the Vivaha saham on NATAL marriage questions too. If
  you want sahams annual-only, the planner needs a varshaphal-scope gate — your
  call (see docs/sahams_pending_derivation_S163.md).


====================================================================
PART 1 — GENERAL RESULTS DOCTRINE  (applies to EVERY saham)
====================================================================

[[seg: sah_gen_fructify | domains: * ]]
WHEN A SAHAM FRUCTIFIES. A saham's result ripens in the DASHA (operating
period) of the saham's LORD (the lord of the sign the saham falls in). This is
the agreed view; a second method times it by the saham-lord's degrees
(navamsa). If the saham-lord's dasha runs within the indicated span, the result
comes in that dasha.
  Nila l.3729–3744: "सहमेश की दशा हो तब फल होगा, यह सर्वसंमत है … जो दिन मिलें
  हैं, यदि उनके भीतर तत्स्वामी की दशा ही हो तो दशा ही में फल हो जायगा।"

[[seg: sah_gen_strength | domains: * ]]
STRENGTH OF A SAHAM. A saham is STRONG when its lord is well placed — in its
own sign / exaltation / a good (kendra-trikona) house — and carries
panchavargeeya strength not less than five, and is NOT in a dusthana (6/8/12).
A saham whose lord is benefic, conjunct or aspected by benefics, and itself
strong, gives the GOOD results of its significations; a saham whose lord is
weak/afflicted is barren.
  Nila l.3764–3775: "जो सहम अपने स्वामी शुभ … से युक्त वा दृष्ट हो तथा सहमेश
  … बली हो तो … शुभ फल का संभव करता है"; l.3781 "पंचवर्गी में पांच से कम न हो
  तथा दुःस्थान में न हो।"

[[seg: sah_gen_halfyear | domains: * ]]
BENEFIC/MALEFIC ASSOCIATION SETS THE HALF-YEAR. (Second half of the year =
from six months after the varsha-pravesh.)
  • malefic-conjunct BUT benefic-aspected → first half bad, second half good.
  • benefic-conjunct BUT malefic-aspected → first half good, second half bad.
  • malefic-conjunct AND malefic-aspected → the whole year bad.
  • benefic-conjunct AND benefic-aspected → the whole year good.
  Nila l.3787–3801 (śloka 33): "पापयुक्त शुभदृष्ट … पूर्वार्ध में अशुभ उत्तरार्ध
  में शुभ … शुभयुक्त पापदृष्ट … पूर्वार्ध शुभ उत्तरार्ध अशुभ …।"

[[seg: sah_gen_punya_primary | domains: * ]]
PUNYA IS THE CHIEF SAHAM. Even if other sahams are benefic, they do not deliver
their good results unless PUNYA is sound. Therefore Punya saham is the FIRST and
principal consideration in every birth-year.
  Nila l.3791–3795 (śloka 34): "सहम शुभ भी हो तो शुभ फल नहीं देते, इस कारण
  पुण्यसहम सभी जन्मवर्ष में मुख्य विचार्य है।"

[[seg: sah_gen_punya_afflicted | domains: spirituality, wealth ]]
PUNYA AFFLICTED. If Punya saham falls in the 6th, 8th or 12th from lagna and is
malefic-conjunct in the year, and its lord is combust (dagdha / astangata), it
destroys dharma, wealth and happiness.
  Nila l.3803–3808 (śloka 35): "जन्म में पुण्यसहम लग्न से छठा आठवां वा बारहवां
  हो और वर्ष में पापयुत हो तथा सहमेश दग्ध अस्तंगत हो तो धर्म, धन और सुख का नाश।"

[[seg: sah_gen_inauspicious_reversed | domains: * ]]
THE REVERSAL RULE (critical). In the aforementioned manner, one should examine
all the sahams in both the natal and annual charts: when the Punya saham is
strong, there is gain of wealth and the like. However, when the Roga (disease),
Ari (enemy), Kali (strife), and Mrityu (death) sahams are strong, they yield the
opposite [inauspicious] result, corresponding to their names. If these five
inauspicious sahams — beginning with Roga — are weak, one should predict
auspicious results in the annual chart and other periods.
  Nila l.3826–3833 (śloka 36): "पुण्यसहम के बलवान् होने में द्रव्यादि लाभ होता
  है परंतु रोग अरि कलि मृत्यु इन सहमों के बलवान् होने में विपरीत फल … रोगादि
  पांच अनिष्ट सहम [यदि] निर्बल [हों] तो वर्षादि में शुभ फल जानना।"

PART 2 — PER-SAHAM SIGNIFICANCE AND RESULTS

Each entry: SIGNIFICATION | FORMULA (plain English; the engine is authority) |

[[seg: sah_punya | domains: spirituality ]]
PUNYA (पुण्य) — merit, fortune, religious good. The chief saham (see
sah_gen_punya_primary). FORMULA day (Moon − Sun) + Lagna; night (Sun − Moon) +
Lagna. STATUS confirmed. PHALA: see sah_gen_punya_afflicted; Punya strong and
well-placed is the foundation of a good year.

[[seg: sah_vidya | domains: education ]]
VIDYA / GURU (विद्या) — learning, knowledge, the preceptor. FORMULA day
(Sun − Moon) + Lagna; night reversed. STATUS confirmed. Raman "Guru = Preceptor".

[[seg: sah_yasas | domains: career ]]
YASAS / KIRTHI (यश) — fame, reputation. FORMULA (Jupiter − Punya) + Lagna.
STATUS confirmed. PHALA: if the Yasas-saham lord is in the 8th house, it destroys
fame and the native becomes involved in one of the great self-committed sins
(mahapatakas). When that same lord is in the 8th, conjoined with and aspected by
malefics, and also combust, it destroys the inherited fame passed down through
the ancestral lineage (father, grandfather, etc.), ruining the reputation of the
entire family. When in ithasala with a benefic → growth of fame, victory in war,
and gain of vehicles; when in ithasala with a malefic → disgrace and loss of
wealth.
  Nila l.3874–3882 (ślokas 40–41): "यशसहमाधीश अष्टम स्थान में … यश का नाश …
  अपने सारे वंश की कीर्ति नाशक है … शुभ इत्थशाली → यश की वृद्धि, युद्ध में जय,
  वाहन लाभ; पापे → अयश, अर्थनाश।"

[[seg: sah_mitra | domains: * ]]
MITRA (मित्र) — friends, alliances. FORMULA day (Jupiter − Punya) + Venus;
night (Punya − Jupiter) + Venus. STATUS confirmed. (No dedicated phala block;
judged by the general doctrine. Tagged broad — no "friends" life-area domain.)

[[seg: sah_pitru | domains: parents ]]
PITRU (पितृ) — father. FORMULA (Saturn − Sun) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed. PHALA:
Pitru saham conjunct/aspected by benefic or own-lord, in good ithasala → wealth,
growth, honour and happiness THROUGH the father. [If the Pitru-saham lord is
weak/afflicted] and in a movable sign → the father dies in a foreign land; if in
a fixed sign → he dies in his own country. When the Pitru-saham lord is in full
strength (more than 15 vishvas) and placed in an auspicious house → one becomes
like a king and enjoys growth of fame. The same is to be understood for the
Matru (mother) saham.
  Nila l.3883–3896, l.3966 (ślokas ~47–49): "पितृसंबंधी धन वृद्धि मान सुख …
  चर राशि में हो तो पिता विदेश में मरे, स्थिर राशि में स्वदेश में मरे।"

[[seg: sah_matru | domains: parents ]]
MATRU (मातृ) — mother. FORMULA (Moon − Venus) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed.
PHALA: judged exactly as Pitru, read for the mother ("मातृसहम में भी ऐसा ही
जानना", Nila l.3968).

[[seg: sah_rajya | domains: career ]]
RAJYA / RAJA (राज्य) — authority, position, political standing. FORMULA
(Saturn − Sun) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed. Raman "Raja = Political". PHALA: when
afflicted (cruel/malefic association, malefic ithasala) → loss of kingdom /
authority / position, and loss of wealth such as gold and the like.
  Nila l.4018–4022: "राज्यनाश … सुवर्णादि द्रव्यका नाश करते है।"

[[seg: sah_putra | domains: children ]]
PUTRA (पुत्र) — children. FORMULA (Jupiter − Moon) + Lagna (day or night same in
Raman). STATUS confirmed. Raman "Putra = Children"; cf. Putra saham in house 5
→ the native gets a child.

[[seg: sah_jeeva | domains: longevity ]]
JEEVA (जीव) — life, vitality, livelihood. FORMULA day (Saturn − Jupiter) + Lagna;
night (Jupiter − Saturn) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed. Raman "Jeeva = Life".

[[seg: sah_karma | domains: career ]]
KARMA / VYAPARA-of-Raman (कर्म) — work, trade, business, profession. FORMULA
(Mars − Mercury) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed. NOTE: Raman and Nilakantha both call
THIS (Mars − Mercury) formula "Vyapara"; JHora's separate "Vyapara" saham is a
DIFFERENT formula (Mars − Saturn) — see sah_vyapara_jhora. PHALA: Karma saham
in malefic ithasala → destruction of work; conjunct OR aspected by Saturn →
especially destroys the career; dagdha (here "पापान्तर्गत" — hemmed between
malefics / papakartari) or retrograde (vakra) → likewise gives adverse results.
  Nila l.4015–4020: "कर्म की विकलता (नाश) … शनि से युक्त वा [दृष्ट] हो तो विशेष
  करके कर्म का नाश।"

[[seg: sah_vivaha | domains: marriage ]]
VIVAHA (विवाह) — marriage. FORMULA (Venus − Saturn) + Lagna (day or night same).
STATUS confirmed. Raman "Vivaha = Marriage". PHALA: Vivaha saham conjunct
own-lord or a benefic, aspected, and in ithasala with a benefic → brings about
marriage; mixed benefic+malefic → marriage with difficulty.
  Nila l.3850–3856 (śloka 39): "विवाहसहम स्वस्वामी वा शुभग्रह युक्त … शुभग्रह के
  साथ इत्थशाली … विवाह प्राप्ति करेगा।"

[[seg: sah_paradara | domains: marriage ]]
PARADARA (परदार) — illicit union, another's spouse. FORMULA (Venus − Sun) +
Lagna. STATUS confirmed. (Inauspicious-leaning; judge with the reversal rule.)

[[seg: sah_satru | domains: enemies_conflict ]]
SATRU (शत्रु) — enemy, foe. FORMULA day (Mars − Saturn) + Lagna; night reversed.
STATUS confirmed. One of the FIVE INAUSPICIOUS sahams → apply the reversal rule
(strong = more enmity, weak = relief). Raman "Satru = Foe or Enemy".

[[seg: sah_kali | domains: enemies_conflict ]]
KALI (कलि) — strife, quarrel, discord. FORMULA (Jupiter − Mars) + Lagna. STATUS
confirmed. One of the FIVE INAUSPICIOUS sahams. PHALA: Kali saham conjunct both
benefic and malefic and in ithasala with a malefic → death amid conflict;
conjunct/aspected by benefic → quick victory in strife; equal benefic+malefic
aspect → only toil and struggle, no decisive win or loss.
  Nila l.3843–3849 (śloka 38): "कलि सहम पाप शुभ दोनों ही से युक्त … पापग्रह से
  इत्थशाली → कलह में मरण; शुभग्रह से युक्त वा दृष्ट → थोड़े ही कलह में जय।"

[[seg: sah_satru_mahatmya | domains: career ]]
MAHATMYA (माहात्म्य) — greatness, dignity, eminence. FORMULA (Punya − Mars) +
Lagna. STATUS confirmed. Judge by general doctrine (status/dignity ≈ career).

[[seg: sah_asha | domains: * ]]
ASHA (आशा) — desire, hope; also "direction". FORMULA (Saturn − Mars) + Lagna.
STATUS confirmed. Nila gloss l.4045: "आशा इच्छा का नाम है, दिशा का भी नाम है।"
(No life-area domain — tagged broad.)

[[seg: sah_sraddha | domains: spirituality ]]
SRADDHA (श्रद्धा) — faith, devotion to dharma; here, trustworthiness/belief.
FORMULA (Venus − Mars) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed. Nila gloss l.4046: "श्रद्धा
धर्मकार्य की मति … यहां विश्वासता का अर्थ लेना मुख्य है।"

[[seg: sah_bandhu | domains: siblings ]]
BANDHU (बन्धु) — kinsmen, relatives (up to seven generations / sapinda). FORMULA
(Mercury − Moon) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed. Nila gloss l.4049: "बांधवः सपिण्ड
सातपुरुष पर्यन्त का नाम है।"

[[seg: sah_vanik | domains: wealth ]]
VANIK (वणिक्) — trade, mercantile business (involving truth and falsehood /
profit and loss — the buying-and-selling character of commerce). FORMULA
(Moon − Mercury) + Lagna. STATUS confirmed. Nila gloss l.4050: "सत्यालीक =
वणिग्भाव" (satyalika = the mercantile nature).

[[seg: sah_bandhana | domains: enemies_conflict ]]
BANDHANA (बन्धन) — bondage, imprisonment. FORMULA (Punya − Saturn) + Lagna.
STATUS confirmed. One of the inauspicious set. PHALA: conjunct own-lord or
benefic and aspected → no fear of prison/bondage; malefic-conjunct/aspected or
in malefic ithasala → bondage.
  Nila l.3975–3980 (śloka 51): "बन्धनसहम स्वस्वामी वा शुभग्रह युक्त दृष्टि हो तो
  बंधन (कारागार) का भय नहीं; पापयुक्त वीक्षित … बंधन।"

[[seg: sah_jadya | domains: health ]]
JADYA (जाड्य) — dullness, inertia, torpor/chronic infirmity; ignorance.
FORMULA day (Mars − Saturn) + Mercury; night reversed. STATUS confirmed. Nila
gloss (l.4043 region): associated with ignorance / forgetfulness / dullness of
body and mind.

[[seg: sah_sastra | domains: education ]]
SASTRA / ŚĀSTRA (शास्त्र) — scripture, learning, knowledge of śruti-smriti.
FORMULA day (Jupiter − Saturn) + Mercury; night (Saturn − Jupiter) + Mercury.
STATUS confirmed (PROMOTED S163; Nila #22, l.3527–28). Nila gloss l.4038:
"शास्त्र, श्रुति, स्मृति ज्ञान।"

[[seg: sah_santapa | domains: health ]]
SANTAPA (संताप) — anguish, mental affliction, sorrow/burning. FORMULA
(Saturn − Moon) + the 6th-bhava cusp; night reversed. STATUS confirmed
(PROMOTED S163; Nila #34, l.3560). Inauspicious. Nila gloss l.4047: "आधि
(मानसी व्यथा), व्याधि, रोग, ताप, संताप — ये सब [संताप] के पर्याय हैं।" (mental
pain, disease, affliction — all synonyms).

[[seg: sah_karyasiddhi | domains: career ]]
KARYASIDDHI (कार्यसिद्धि) — accomplishment of undertakings, success in effort.
FORMULA day (Saturn − Sun) + lord-of-Sun's-sign; NIGHT (Saturn − Moon) +
lord-of-Moon's-sign [day/night-DISTINCT]. STATUS confirmed (PROMOTED S163; Nila
#31, l.3530). PHALA: Karyasiddhi saham conjunct/aspected by benefics, or in
ithasala with a benefic → victory in a contest/undertaking; fully benefic →
great success; mixed with malefics → success only through struggle. The same
mode of judging applies to Vivaha and the rest.
  Nila l.3836–3840 (śloka 37): "कार्यसिद्धि सहम शुभ[ग्रह] से युक्त वा दृष्ट …
  संग्राम में जय देता है … मिश्र … तो हार-जीत मिली हुई।"

[[seg: sah_artha | domains: wealth ]]
ARTHA / DHANA (धन) — wealth, acquisition. FORMULA (2nd house cusp − 2nd-house
lord) + Lagna, no night reversal. STATUS confirmed (Nilakantha Dhana #26 +
reproduces JHora on 4 charts). PHALA: if conjoined with and aspected by benefics,
and also in ithasala with a benefic → the native loses/spends previously
accumulated wealth, then re-accumulates wealth through their own effort along
with happiness; if conjoined with and aspected exclusively by malefics → total
loss of wealth. Aspected by an enemy [planet] → fear of enemies and loss of
wealth through thieves, etc.; aspected by or conjoined with a friendly [planet]
→ gain of wealth, honour, fame, and happiness.
  Nila l.3912–3922 (ślokas 45–46): "रिपुदृष्ट्या रिपोर्भीतिः तस्करादे धनक्षयः ॥
  मित्रदृष्ट्या मित्रयोगाद् धनं मानो यशः सुखम्।"

[[seg: sah_labha | domains: wealth ]]
LABHA (लाभ) — gain, profit. FORMULA (11th house cusp − 11th-house lord) + Lagna,
no night reversal. STATUS confirmed (house-lord family, exact parallel of Dhana;
reproduces JHora on 4 charts). The 11th is the bhava of gains, so a strong,
benefic Labha saham → fulfilment of desire and profit.

[[seg: sah_gaurava | domains: career ]]
GAURAVA / GURUTA (गौरव / गुरुता) — honour, eminence, respect. FORMULA
(Jupiter − Moon) + Sun; night reversed (swap A,B). STATUS confirmed (Nilakantha
Guruta #45 + reproduces JHora on 4 charts). PHALA: conjunct own-lord/benefic and
aspected → happiness, royal eminence, fame, prosperity; benefic ithasala →
wealth, vehicles, fame; malefic-conjunct or malefic ithasala → loss of position
(authority), loss of wealth, AND loss of happiness. Half-year split per the
general rule.
  Nila l.3998–4012 (śloka 53).

[[seg: sah_bhratru | domains: siblings ]]
BHRATRU (भ्रातृ) — brother(s). FORMULA (Jupiter − Saturn) + Lagna, NO night
reversal. STATUS confirmed (reproduces JHora on 4 charts; JHora tradition — the
name is not in Nilakantha's 50). Judge by the general doctrine for siblings.

[[seg: sah_vyapara_jhora | domains: wealth ]]
VYAPARA-of-JHora (व्यापार, JHora sense) — trade/business. FORMULA (Mars − Saturn)

From B.V. Raman, "Varshaphal or The Hindu Progressed Horoscope", Ch.VII §64–76.
Zero translation risk — reproduce as-is for the Raman-cited core.

§64 SAHAMS — special positions corresponding to Bhavas. "These are 50 according
to Nilakantha, 48 according to Venkatesa, and 21 according to Kesava." General
rule: to find Punya Saham deduct the Sun's longitude from the Moon's (if the
year commences by day; vice-versa by night). If there is no ascendant between
the position of minuend and subtrahend, 30° are added to the result; then the
longitude of the ascendant is always added.

§65 PUNYA (Religious): day Moon − Sun; night Sun − Moon.
§66 GURU (Vidya): day Sun − Moon; night Moon − Sun.
§67 KIRTHI (Yasas): day Jupiter − Punya Saham; night Punya − Guru.
§68 MITRA: day (Guru Saham − Punya Saham) + Sukra; night (Punya + Guru) + Sukra.
§69 RAJA (Rajya): day Saturn − Sun; night Sun − Saturn.
§70 PUTRA: day or night Guru (Jupiter) − Chandra (Moon).
§71 JEEVA: day Saturn − Guru; night Guru − Saturn.
§72 VYAPARA (= our Karma): day Kuja (Mars) − Budha (Mercury); night reversed.
§73 VIVAHA: day or night Sukra (Venus) − Sani (Saturn).
§74 SATRU: day Kuja − Sani; night Sani − Kuja.
§75 EXPLANATIONS: Punya = Religious/merit, Guru = Preceptor, Kirthi = Fame,
    Mitra = Friend, Raja = Political, Putra = Children, Jeeva = Life,
    Vyapara = Trade or Business, Vivaha = Marriage, Satru = Foe or Enemy.
§76 LORDS OF SAHAMS: the lord of the rasi in which a saham's longitude falls is
    the lord of that saham.
