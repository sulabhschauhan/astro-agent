## Verdict mapping (gate contract unchanged downstream)

    SATISFIED    -> APPLICABLE
    CONTRADICTED -> NOT_APPLICABLE   (the only drop authority)
    UNEVALUABLE  -> UNDETERMINED     (kept, hedged, never dropped)

## Deliberate design points

- NO FALLBACK to the prose reader on a typed UNEVALUABLE: re-running the regex
  could OVERTURN a typed verdict and reintroduce the S125 wrong-drop classes.
- A claim with no preconditions goes to the prose reader exactly as before.
- An illegal precondition NEVER costs the claim; the ghost guard remains the
  only thing in the interpreter that discards one.
- New metrics: `typed_decided`, `prose_decided`, `typed_share_pct`,
  `predicate_coverage` (per fact class). Measurements only; nothing branches on them.
