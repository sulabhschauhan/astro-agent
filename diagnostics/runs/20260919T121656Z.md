## Findings raised by this batch

1. **`_naabhasa_sankhya` contradicts its own chapter.** ch35 v17: "None of
   these seven yogas will be operable, if another Nabhasa yoga explained
   earlier is derivable." The existing row fires unconditionally. This batch
   does **not** rewrite that verdict (it is oracle-asserted in `test_rules.py`);
   it emits `naabhasa_sankhya_suppressed` alongside, naming the blockers, so
   the suppression is visible and the sankhya verdict stays auditable.
   **Needs a ruling:** leave as two rows, or suppress the sankhya row directly.
2. **Chandra-Mangal yoga is not in BPHS.** It belongs to Jataka Parijata /
   Phaladeepika. The retrieval corpus is BPHS only, so a row for it could cite
   no verse and every claim resting on it would be dropped by the ghost guard.
   Not implemented — out of corpus, not out of scope.
3. **ch15 v3 translation divergence.** R. Santhanam's English reads "the 5th
   lord"; the sloka reads सुखस्थानाधिप — the lord of the 4th. Resolved to the
   Sanskrit (v2 and v4 either side are both 4th-house rules); the divergence is
   recorded in the row's own evidence rather than silently taken.
4. **ch35 v11 (Vajra / Yava) reads as an OR in translation.** Taken literally,
   Vajra fires on any chart with Jupiter and Venus angular, whatever the other
   five grahas do — which no *aakriti* ("shape") yoga can mean; every
   neighbouring verse says "all the planets". Implemented with both arms
   required and the seven grahas confined to the angles. Recorded, and tested
   with an explicit negative for the literal reading.
5. **ch19 vv.8, 11, 15 and the third arm of v9 are not implemented.** vv.8/15
   and v9's third arm turn on the ascendant lord being "bereft of strength" /
   "exceedingly strong"; no strength fact exists (Shadbala is not computed) and
   a dignity label is not a strength proxy — building on one would ship P-032
   again. v11 needs the 8th lord *debilitated in the ascendant*, which
   Santhanam's own note to v12 shows is unreachable by whole signs; a rule that
   can never fire is the `_GRAHAS`/nodes defect over again, so it was not
   written rather than written dead.
6. **The four pre-existing PVR-sourced rows still carry no sloka id** —
   `_vesi`, `_sunaphaa`, `_anaphaa`, `_adhi` (and `_naabhasa_sankhya`). The new
   rows cite BPHS directly, so the batch leaves the module inconsistent.
   Retrofitting is behaviour-visible (their evidence lists are asserted in
   `test_rules.py`) and was deliberately **not** bundled here.
