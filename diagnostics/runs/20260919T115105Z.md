# S138 Batch 1 -- Dhana + Daridra yogas (BPHS 1 ch41, ch42) (run 20260919T115105Z)

## Why these first

Web research on what users actually ask (career, money, property, love,
marriage, travel, children, education) vs what the detector computes:
coverage was DEEP on career/status (Raja, Vipareeta, PMP, 4 Yogadas) and
ZERO on money, marriage, children, property and travel. Money is the 2nd
commonest question and had no yoga at all.
ch42 also gives the catalogue its FIRST HONEST NEGATIVES -- every yoga
before this was good news, which is why answers read as flattery
(SESSION_LOG S136 s12 item 6).

## Spec source

bphs1_ch41 'Combinations For Wealth' and bphs1_ch42 'Combinations For
Penury', read from data/chapter_index_bphs.json -- the corpus itself.
Every rule cites its sloka in evidence (test-enforced). No formula from
an oracle (P-028); no doctrine prose in any reason (P-029, test-enforced).

  (5th lord in 5th + 11th lord in 11th) + 7 sign-locked specifics.

  aspected by the named supporters (both arms of the disjunction).

  vv.16-18 -- Mars+Saturn in the 2nd AND its reversal to great wealth

## Deliberately NOT built, with reasons (so no session re-derives them)

- ch41 vv.18-34 divisional-amsa dignities: needs Dasa-Varga amsa dignity,
  not in the fact block. Not synthesisable.
- ch41 v16 (5th/9th lords give wealth): a DEFINITION true of every chart;
  it would fire for everyone and read as a Barnum line (S119 invariant 3).
- ch42 vv.2,3,7b,8,10,11,13,14,15: every one turns on a MARAKA lord and
  ch42 does not define maraka. Taking '2nd and 7th lords' from elsewhere
  would be a formula from a non-spec source (P-028). Build when sourced.
- ch42 v5: needs 'an enemy's sign' = friendship tiers, deliberately OUT
  of the fact block as contested (S130). Not approximated.
- ch42 v9, v12: need dispositor chains / an auspicious-house taxonomy
  the fact block does not define.
