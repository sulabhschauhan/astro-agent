# S140 measurement — chart-conditioned `plan.houses` selection (Tier 1.1)

OFFLINE, no LLM, no code edit. Cloud reproduction of the S139 deterministic
selection path (`select_units` -> `build_payload` -> `filter_segments_by_domain`)
against every staged `qa_capture` turn, then the candidate filter applied and
checked against the actually-cited segments of the SAME turn.

Reproduction fidelity: offline `kept_segments` matches the live capture exactly
(career turn 303 == capture `segments_kept: 303`).

CANDIDATE FILTER (the only design that adds NO new tagging hierarchy, per P-034):
  declared = union of house numbers in a segment's extracted relation pairs.
  DROP iff declared non-empty AND declared ∩ plan.houses == empty. Fail-safe:
  a segment with no extractable relation is KEPT.

## Two findings, both disqualifying

1. NEGLIGIBLE CUT ON THE COSTLY TURNS. Aggregate drop 1.34% of payload tokens;
   0.1–0.2% on the 84k–150k life-subject turns. The payload is 75.3% relation-LESS
   fail-safe-kept segments + 16.7% whole-chapter units = ~92% untouchable by ANY
   relation/house filter, and those tokens sit in the wide life-subject domains
   the S137 standing warning forbids narrowing.

2. IT DROPS CITED VERSES — fails the "drops no cited verse" bar. 10 cited drops
   across the sample, concentrated on the small-domain turns where the filter cuts
   most (wealth/education/travel, 5–8%). Root cause is doctrinal, not tuning: a
   verse is indexed by its "lord of A in B" placement, which is a DIFFERENT
   coordinate system from the question's houses. Verified examples:
     - travel (houses 9,12) cited ch24_s103 "11th lord in the 6th ... living in
       foreign places" — relation {6,11}, dropped. The MOST on-topic verse.
     - wealth (houses 2,11) cited ch24_s053 "6th lord in the 6th ... enmity with
       kinsmen" — relation {6}, dropped.
     - education (houses 4,5,9) cited ch24_s108 "11th lord in the 12th" — {1,11,12}, dropped.

## Conclusion
Wiring `plan.houses` into segment selection is NOT the dasha-scaling lever and is
NOT safe. The chart-relation filter (`run_filter`) already does the only sound
chart-conditioning (does the placement match the native's chart). `plan.houses`
(query houses) is not a superset of the houses the useful verses declare.

## Real headroom question for dasha (from the decomposition)
Costly turns already reach 140–150k against the 150k ceiling on MULTI-DOMAIN
questions; 92% of that is relation-less life-subject segments + whole chapters.
The lever is one of: (a) per-domain / multi-domain selection budgeting, (b) the
S136 "segment-split" direction, (c) a genuinely different relevance signal — which
is the tagging hierarchy S138/P-034 ruled out and needs Sulabh's call. NOT houses.
