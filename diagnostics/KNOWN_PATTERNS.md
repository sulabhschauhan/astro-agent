## Standing rules (not bug patterns)

- **Never set a retrieval/extraction tuning number from a hand-picked or
  synthetic sample.** Collect real production failures first (the dogfood
  capture net), set thresholds from that evidence. Ruling: S83 (parked
  `_N_RESULTS_PER_FEATURE` widening pending real silences), reaffirmed S84
  (live data vindicated window=3; widening stays parked).
- **"Absent from top-N search results" is not "missing from the corpus."**
  Establish existence by direct `collection.get(ids=[...])` lookup, never by
  whether a chunk appears in ranked output. Conflating these produced a
  false DATA verdict and three sessions of wrong direction (S81).
